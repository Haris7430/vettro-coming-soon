About Us

The Vision Behind Vettro.

"Built over two decades of experience in interior materials and execution."

Our journey began in 2002, with the foundation of Venice Furnishings and Venice Metals, focusing on interior decor materials and aluminium systems.

In 2011, we expanded into glass & plywood solutions with the launch of Real Glass & Paints, serving residential and commercial projects.

By 2019, we brought everything together under Vettro Traders & Integrated Services, along with V-Decor, to offer a complete, factory-backed interior ecosystem — from materials to execution.

Today, Vettro operates as a unified platform built on experience, capability, and responsibility.
